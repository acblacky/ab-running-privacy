# AB Running Support

AB Running provides personalised running coaching for runners of all abilities and all paces.

## Need Help?

If you need help with AB Running, your account, your training plan, or another aspect of the app, please contact:

**Email:** abrunner11111@hotmail.com

We aim to respond to support enquiries as soon as reasonably possible.

## Account and App Support

You can contact us for help with:

- Signing in or accessing your account
- Your personalised training plan
- Recording or viewing training
- Coach feedback
- Races and personal bests
- Running history
- Notifications
- Account deletion
- Other technical or account issues

## Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your AB Running account directly within the app:

**Profile → Account → Delete Account**

If you have difficulty accessing this option, contact us using the support email above.
