# AB Running – Account and Data Deletion

AB Running respects your privacy and your right to request deletion of your account and personal data.

## How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your AB Running account and associated data:

1. Email abrunning11111@gmail.com.
2. Use the subject line **AB Running – Account Deletion Request**.
3. Send the request from the email address associated with your AB Running account.

We may need to verify that you own the account before processing your request.

## What data will be deleted?

When your request is processed, we will delete your AB Running account and associated personal data, including your profile information, training plans, running activity records, personal bests, and coaching messages, where held.

## Data retention

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, including relevant financial or accounting records. Any retained information will be kept only for the applicable legal retention period.

## Questions

For questions about account or data deletion, contact abrunning11111@gmail.com.
